The Rollout Trap

Twelve invented clinics, one invented telehealth program, and an invented true effect that you can see here and that no real analyst ever gets to see. Nothing you do is saved or sent.

Each clinic has a built-in disadvantage score that lowers first-session attendance whether or not it offers telehealth, and clinics with lower scores also improve faster over time for reasons unrelated to the program. The program truly adds 5 percentage points of attendance at any clinic that has it. Pick how clinics get the program, estimate the effect from the data, and repeat 300 times to see how the estimates are spread. When a design works, the spread centers on 5.
