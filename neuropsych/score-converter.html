Labels for normally distributed tests are assigned from the standard score rounded to a whole number, the form in which scores are reported. T, scaled, and z scores are labeled through their standard-score equivalent. Performance validity tests are not converted here. AACN labels them valid range, indeterminate range, or invalid range scores against the test's own cutoffs.
