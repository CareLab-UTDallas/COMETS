Score a design-recall protocol

An examinee studies a page of six figures and draws them from memory. Each record below gives the scorer's two decisions for every figure, whether the drawing met the written accuracy criteria and whether it is in its own position on the page. You turn those decisions into points, then into the summary scores.

Nothing here is saved. Every examinee and every record is invented. Practice as often as you like.

Each figureOne point if the drawing meets the written accuracy criteria for that figure. One point if it is in that figure's own position. The two points are awarded independently, so a figure scores 0, 1, or 2.
Each trialSix figures, 0 to 12.
Total RecallTrial 1 plus Trial 2 plus Trial 3. The delayed trial is not included.
LearningThe higher of Trial 2 and Trial 3, minus Trial 1.
Percent RetainedDelayed recall divided by the higher of Trial 2 and Trial 3, times 100. Round to a whole number here.
DiscriminationRecognition hits minus recognition false alarms.

Rules are paraphrased from the sources cited on the Canvas page. Use the manual during a real administration.
