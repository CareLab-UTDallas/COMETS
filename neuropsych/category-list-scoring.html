3. Semantic clustering, learning trials

Count a cluster each time a correct word immediately follows another correct word from the same category. A repetition or intrusion between them breaks the pair. Expected clustering for a trial is (correct − 1) / 5, and the chance-adjusted score is observed minus expected. Average the chance-adjusted scores over the trials with at least 2 correct words, and give the average to one decimal place.
