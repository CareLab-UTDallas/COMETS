Practice start, reverse, and discontinue decisions on the WAIS-IV

Each case names a subtest, gives its rules, and shows the item scores so far in the order you gave the items. Decide what happens next. The reasoning appears after every choice, so a wrong answer teaches as much as a right one.

Nothing here is saved. The cases use rule facts only and contain no test items. Practice as often as you like.

Rules are paraphrased from the WAIS-IV Administration and Scoring Manual (Wechsler, 2008), chapter 2 and Table 2.7. Use the manual during a real administration.
