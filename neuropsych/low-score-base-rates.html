Low scores in healthy people

Design a battery and see how many healthy examinees would obtain one, two, or more low scores on it. The tool simulates 20,000 healthy people from a statistical model in which every pair of tests correlates at the level you set. It simulates. It is not a norm table for any real battery, whose tests correlate unevenly and whose scores are not perfectly normal.

Nothing here is saved. The same settings always give the same result, because the simulation uses a fixed seed.

Scores are simulated as z scores. A score is low when it falls below the cutoff you chose.
