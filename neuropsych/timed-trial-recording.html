From seconds to T score

  1. Read each hand's scaled score from its time in seconds. The writing hand is read in the dominant-hand column and the other hand in the nondominant-hand column. A faster time gives a higher scaled score.
  2. Choose Appendix D or Appendix E by the examinee's race. Then, on that appendix's page for the examinee's sex, years of education, and age, read the T score in the same row as the scaled score, in that hand's column.
