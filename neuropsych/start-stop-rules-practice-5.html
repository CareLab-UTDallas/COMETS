Practice start, reverse, and discontinue decisions

Each case names a subtest by what it asks of the examinee (the Canvas page gives the published names), gives its rules, and shows the item scores so far in the order you gave the items. Decide what happens next. The reasoning appears after every choice, so a wrong answer teaches as much as a right one. The last two cases come from the memory battery.
