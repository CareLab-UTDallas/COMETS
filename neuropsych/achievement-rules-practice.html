Basal, discontinue, and raw score

Each record is an invented examinee’s item scores, in item numbers only. Decide where the record stops, whether the basal is met, what earlier items receive credit, and the total raw score. The reason appears after every answer.

Nothing here is saved. The records are invented, contain no test items, and change each time you draw a new one.
