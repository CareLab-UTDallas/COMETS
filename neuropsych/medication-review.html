Review a medication list before you interpret

Each invented case gives a medication list, the timing of the last doses, and what happened during testing. Make three decisions. Name the drug classes and their likely effects, flag any concern from the older-adult guidance, and choose the next step. The reasoning appears after every choice.

Nothing here is saved. All people and lists are invented. Nothing here is medical advice, and no examinee is ever told to start, stop, or change a medicine.

    The rules are paraphrased from the sources cited on the Canvas page, including the 2023 AGS Beers Criteria and the NICE delirium guideline. Your supervisor and the prescriber make every real decision.
