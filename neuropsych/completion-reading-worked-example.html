Instructions for this activity

Worked Example. Reading Sentence Completions Under a Referral Question

About 20 minutes

This builds on the Sentence Completion page, where Ms. Brewster's first eight completions appear. Here the content reading itself is the focus, and the problem is the reader. Every stem and completion is invented for training. None comes from a published form.

Stage 1. Learn from the Expert

Read Ms. Brewster's fourteen completions and watch an experienced reader work through them under the referral question "rule out depression." The reader writes the hypothesis first, marks what fits, marks what cuts against it, and ends with a hypothesis and a way to check it. Read the amber annotations. Then use Read under a different referral to see the same completions read under two other questions.

Stage 2. Fill in the Gaps

Select Next Stage: Fill in the Gaps. You read a new invented protocol, Mr. Treadwell's, referred for worry and sleep problems, and do the reader's work yourself in two gaps. A third gap shows you the same man's completions written later, when he had something at stake. For each gap, write your reading, select Submit Response, and read the Expert Response, Technique, and Feedback.

You are done when you have submitted all three gaps and read each comparison.

Read and engage actively. The ideas here return in this module's knowledge checks. Nothing to submit.

What it adds to your clinical thinking. Confirmation bias and impression management, seen in action on the same page of writing, so that you recognize both when you meet a content reading in a record.
