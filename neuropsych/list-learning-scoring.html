Score a word-list protocol

Each examinee below is invented, and so is the word list, which was written for this practice and is not the published list. Read what the examinee said on each trial, count the correct words, repetitions, and intrusions, then compute the summary scores and check your work. Keep the scoring rules from the Canvas page open beside you.

Nothing here is saved. These are practice responses, not patient records. The tool scores raw values only and gives no norms.

2. Delay and recognition

Words the examinee accepted on recognition are circled. Count the List A words accepted (hits) and the new words accepted (false positives).

3. Summary scores

Give percentages to one decimal place and memory efficiency to two. A negative interference value means fewer words on the later trial.

Sources. Trial procedure and recording follow the research battery's published worksheets. Trials 1–5 total, sum of trials, percentage retention, recognition percentage correct, memory efficiency, and total intrusions follow Stricker et al. (2021). Learning slope (Trial 5 minus Trial 1) and forgetting follow Moradi et al. (2017). Learning over trials follows Alviarez-Schulze et al. (2022), and recognition discrimination (hits minus false positives) follows Loring et al. (2023). The interference comparisons follow Ebert and Anderson (2009) and are written here as differences.

No normative values are included. Norm-referenced T scores require the published lookup table and formulas in Stricker et al. (2021), which are taught on the Canvas page, not reproduced here.
