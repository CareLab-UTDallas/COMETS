Keep the Presentation Rate

Span and list tasks are read aloud at a fixed rate. Reading too fast or too slow changes the task the norms describe. Hear the model pace, then read invented sequences aloud while you tap each item, and check your timing.

Nothing here is saved. Every sequence is invented and none comes from a test. Practice as often as you like.
