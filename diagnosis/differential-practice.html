Practice the differential decision

Each invented case runs from the interview to the record. Pick the best next question, settle each rule-out, name the feature that decides it, and make the DSM decision. The reason and its DSM source follow every answer.

Nothing here is saved. Cases are invented. DSM points are paraphrased, or quoted where the exact wording matters. A point cited to DSM-5 / DSM-5-TR (APA, 2013, 2022) is verified as unchanged in DSM-5-TR. A point cited to DSM-5 (APA, 2013) alone was not verified against DSM-5-TR. Licensure and the EPPP use DSM-5-TR, so check it before recording.
