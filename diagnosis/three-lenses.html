One case, three lenses

Classify the same invented presentation three ways. Make the DSM decision, mark the HiTOP spectrum or spectra, and place the main mechanism in an RDoC domain and construct. Then compare what each lens adds.

Nothing here is saved. Cases are invented and DSM points are paraphrased. The diagnosis is made in DSM terms. Licensure and the EPPP use DSM-5-TR.

    Domain and construct names follow the NIMH RDoC matrix. HiTOP spectra and subfactors follow the Official HiTOP Working Model (HiTOP Consortium, 2022, CC BY 4.0), with Kotov et al. (2017) as the baseline paper. Somatoform and the Mania links are provisional in that model. Every link from a case observation to an RDoC construct, and every placement marked as course inference, is the course’s reading.
