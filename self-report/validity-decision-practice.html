Decide whether a profile can be interpreted

Each case is an invented validity-scale profile from a 335-item True/False inventory, with T scores for the scales and a raw count of unscorable responses. Work through the decision in order, one question at a time. The reasoning appears after every choice, so a wrong answer teaches as much as a right one.

Nothing here is saved. The profiles are invented and contain no test items. Practice as often as you like.

Thresholds and decision rules are paraphrased from the interpretation chapter of the publisher's manual (Tables 5-2 to 5-12 and pp. 29 to 39, 72), as cited on the Canvas page. Use the manual's full tables during a real interpretation.
