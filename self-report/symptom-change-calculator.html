Reliable and clinically significant change

Each case gives invented totals on a 20-item trauma symptom checklist rated 0 to 4, so totals run from 0 to 80 and a lower total means fewer symptoms. You decide whether a change is larger than measurement error and whether the end score crosses the published threshold. Keep the Canvas page open beside you.

Nothing here is saved. These are practice totals, not patient records. The SD and reliability values in the arithmetic cases are invented.

Sources. Rules are paraphrased from the sources cited on the Canvas page. The two thresholds are published values from a study of treatment-seeking men, and the Canvas page explains their limits.

The tool holds no items and no form content.
