Read a four-point inventory's response style

Each case is an invented validity profile from a 344-item inventory answered on a four-point scale, with the count of omitted items, T scores for four validity scales, and raw scores for four supplemental indexes. Work through the decision in order, one question at a time. The reasoning appears after every choice, so a wrong answer teaches as much as a right one.

Nothing here is saved. The profiles are invented and contain no test items. Practice as often as you like.

Rules are paraphrased from the publisher's manual (2nd ed., pp. 13, 27 to 32), as cited on the Canvas page. Use the manual and the full computer report during a real interpretation.
