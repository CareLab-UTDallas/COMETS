Practice asking about risk

Ten invented intake moments, in a new order each time. In each one you choose the next question, name what the person has told you, and choose what you do under PSEC procedure. Feedback follows every choice, and you can keep choosing until you find the best answer. Your first try is what counts toward your score.

You finished every case

The PSEC rules, once more.
  • Ask directly, in every interview, and bring anything positive to your supervisor before the person leaves.
  • In an emergency, notify your supervisor immediately, or the PSEC Director if your supervisor is unavailable. Call 911 for imminent danger.
  • 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, is a resource to give. It never replaces telling your supervisor.
  • Never promise confidentiality, and document right away.
  • PSEC does not admit people with suicidal or homicidal ideation for treatment. Your supervisor handles the referral.

Practice only. These cases are invented, and with a real person your supervisor decides what happens next. The cases include suicidal thoughts, thoughts of harming others, and partner violence, without graphic detail. If you are struggling yourself, call or text 988. Nothing here is saved or sent.
