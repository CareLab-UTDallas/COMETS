Evaluation or Research?

Eight invented clinic projects. For each, decide whether it is program evaluation, quality improvement, or research, and then read what the design can and cannot claim. Classification of a real project belongs to your institution’s IRB. This activity builds the reasoning you bring to that conversation. Nothing you do is saved or sent.

Two questions decide it. Is the work a systematic investigation, and is its primary purpose to develop or contribute to generalizable knowledge? A project that answers a local question for local decisions, with no intent to generalize, is evaluation or quality improvement. A project designed so that its answer should hold beyond this clinic is research, and it needs review before data collection starts. Intent can change midstream, so a plan to publish or present findings as knowledge for other settings changes the classification.
