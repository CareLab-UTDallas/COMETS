Risk classification

Each case is a short invented description of what a client told an interviewer. Classify the most severe type of suicidal ideation and the behavior category for the time frame given, or choose to clarify first when the description leaves the rating open, then read the reason. Keep the Canvas page open beside you.

Nothing here is saved. Every client is invented. If you are having thoughts of suicide yourself, call or text 988, and in an emergency call 911.

The definitions, paraphrased

Ideation, most severe type. 1, a wish to be dead or to sleep and not wake up, with no thoughts of killing oneself. 2, general thoughts of killing oneself, with no method, no intent, and no plan. 3, a method in mind but no worked-out plan and no intention of acting. 4, some intention of acting, without a specific plan. 5, the details of a plan fully or partly worked out, with some intent. Rate the highest type present. Types are never added.

Clarify before rating. When a description leaves out the detail a definition turns on, ask before you rate. If it stays unclear, record what was said and raise it with your supervisor.

Behavior. Actual attempt, a potentially self-injurious act with at least some wish to die, with or without injury. Intent may be inferred when the person thought the act could be lethal. Interrupted attempt, stopped by someone or something outside the person before the act began. Once any part of the act begins, it is an actual attempt. Aborted or self-interrupted attempt, the person takes steps and stops themselves before any self-destructive act. Preparatory act, anything beyond a thought or a statement that prepares for an attempt, such as gathering a means or preparing for death. Non-suicidal self-injury, self-injury done for other reasons with no intention of dying.

Sources. Definitions are paraphrased from the sources cited on the Canvas page. Use the form and the course page during a real administration, and follow the Asking About Risk procedure after any positive answer.

The tool holds no form items and no form content. Each case has one behavior category so that the reason stays clear. Real interviews can have several.
