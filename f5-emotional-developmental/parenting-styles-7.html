Instructions for this activity

Place the Parent

About 10 minutes

Each round draws eight short portraits of invented parents from a larger bank. For each one, place the parent on the two dimensions from the reading. Responsiveness is the warmth side, how attuned and supportive the parent is to the child's needs and feelings. Demandingness is the structure side, the expectations, supervision, and follow-through on discipline. Pick the square of the grid that fits the pattern you see. The feedback then names the parenting style and explains which details in the portrait point to it.

Best done after the reading section on parenting styles. Read for the pattern across many days, not the single most vivid moment. A few portraits include one bad evening or a strict rule on purpose, to test that habit. You'll get a score at the end, and a “Try again” button draws a fresh set. Your first-try accuracy for the session stays on screen until you close the page.

This activity scores only the squares you pick, and nothing is saved or sent. There's nothing to write and nothing to submit.

What it adds to your clinical thinking. Hearing warmth and structure as two separate questions when a person describes how discipline and emotion were handled at home.

Place the Parent

Two questions describe a home's everyday climate. How warm and attuned was the parent? How much did the parent expect, supervise, and follow through? Place each invented parent on both, then see which style the pattern matches and why.
