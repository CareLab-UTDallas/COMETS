Hand score a keyed true-false form

Each case is an invented 30-item true-false form with three scales and its own invented practice key. You turn each response into an item score, total the scales, apply the blank-item rules, and make the screening decision. A third set asks you to find the error in a classmate's scoring sheet. Keep the Canvas page open beside you.

Nothing here is saved. The item numbers, the key, and the cutoffs are invented for practice and change with every case. They are not the key or the cutoffs of any published test. Score a real form only from the key taught on the Canvas page.

Sources. Rules are paraphrased from the sources cited on the Canvas page. Use the manual during a real administration.

The tool holds no items, no item text, and no published scoring key. Every response, key, and cutoff is invented.
