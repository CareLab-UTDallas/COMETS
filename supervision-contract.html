Supervision Contract Tool

Use this tool to draft a written supervision agreement with a research mentor, a clinical supervisor, or a junior trainee you supervise. Work through it with the other person. Each section is a conversation to have, and the contract records what you both agreed.

Everything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved anywhere. Copy or download the text before you close the page.

Do not enter patient, participant, or client information. If you supervise a junior trainee, write that trainee's role and not their name.

Read the text with the other person, make changes, and then both of you sign. The signature lines sit at the bottom of the text.







This tool follows the elements that the American Psychological Association (2014) guidelines for clinical supervision and Gonsalvez (2014) list for a written supervision agreement, together with the feedback-process requirement in Standard 7.06 of the APA Ethics Code. It is a drafting aid. Your program, your site, and your supervisor decide what the agreement must contain.
